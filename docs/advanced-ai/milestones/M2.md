## Architecture and baseline

M1 groups every technology that passes `TechManager.canBeResearched` by its ruleset cost. For a new regular queue it chooses the first group with a positive AI weight and falls back to the first group. If the selected group has multiple choices and another group exists, it also takes the group at index 1. Free technology instead starts from the highest positive-weight group and falls back to the highest cost group, recalculating eligibility for each claim. Both M1 branches use the civilization's state-based weighted draw. The common AI weight scales ruleset `AiChoiceWeight` uniques. Buildings and units can receive additional personality-specific weights. The technology lookup supplies no intrinsic unlock valuation.

The construction evaluator considers only its existing buildable candidates. It computes building yield deltas with a cloned city, scales yields and other effects by personality, credits defense, victory and one-time effects, and includes separately evaluated worker, workboat, army and spaceship candidates. It ranks the resulting value times the ruleset weight per remaining work. The remaining work is actual production cost minus this city's invested work. Queue validation, military resource and supply checks, city emergency selection, and worker and settler rules remain in the normal game flow.

### Shared strategy and lifecycle

`StrategicDirector` computes five soft priorities from own next-turn science, gold and culture, treasury, happiness, city count and damage, military unit count, and the civilization's side of its diplomatic relationships. Personality shifts a priority by up to 0.10. A threatened city or a near-term gold deficit has a larger effect. A lazy `AdvancedAIContext` follows the active AI civilization from turn preparation through cleanup so construction completions and the regular automation pass can reuse the strategic snapshot. The context is restored in a `finally` block and the cache is transient. Queue legality, research cost, city yields and visible threats are checked when used. Diplomacy, units or cities can change after the strategy snapshot, so its weights express a turn-level preference.

### Research

The bounded search evaluates up to 96 targets, 768 prerequisite checks, and four distinct technologies per path. It collects each target's outstanding prerequisites, checks for missing entries, orders the closure by available prerequisite, base cost and name, and rejects cycles and unavailable technologies. `TechManager.canBeResearched` checks the first step against live game state. Estimates for downstream availability use the current conditions. The planner queues a single technology, which allows it to replan once the queue empties. An existing queue and free research continue through the baseline pathways.

Utility includes the allowable ruleset weight and generic yields, military strength, improvements, resource reveals and construction victory milestones. An unlock with several requirements receives credit at its last required step. The cost calculation uses the actual current player-facing research price, invested science and shared overflow once per path. Each step is discounted by completion time and depth, and the full path is discounted by total time. Stable cost and name ordering resolve ties. The synthetic `Number Marks` test selects it for a path through `Portable Records` to `Celestial Measurements`.

The planner estimates construction unlocks from the ruleset and own economic scale. It omits contextual AI-weight conditions unless they depend on own research or era, or public settings. Locational suitability and changes to ruleset availability after hypothetical steps are outside this estimate.

### Production

City roles track production, science, growth, culture, military production and defense. They use own yields, population, capital status and production relative to the civilization average. In a known war they may also use visible hostile military units within three tiles. The existing score is multiplied by one plus the strategy adjustment (bounded to ±16%) and the role adjustment (bounded to ±12%). An active queue and the city emergency route take precedence. Damaged cities bypass the adjustment and a victory candidate that would win under the baseline score keeps its rank. Existing buildability, resource, unit-supply, purchase and overflow mechanics apply.

Optional diagnostics record strategy drivers, the best research paths and their costs and discounted contributions, and base and adjusted production scores. Timers report aggregate strategy and research cost. The pre-existing construction timer does not emit an AI-only span because the normal no-notification branch returns from the enclosing function.

## Ablation and decision

A targeted 64-game screen compared Strategy + City with M1, using M1 technology selection on both sides. All map, rules, player and victory settings matched the main A/B. The overlay SHA-256 was `fe27cb460b072a2ff2c315020e1f92363106c3ae1b39653221fe80bd48681e87`; its JAR SHA-256 was `0990fb37b5c5d2d85a0d545156c21d505f00f83a2587cc943f9a9ee60a264cd5`.

The first attempt was interrupted after 55 of 64 games (32 wins, 23 losses, 0 draws for Strategy + City). The HotSpot error report recorded `EXCEPTION_ACCESS_VIOLATION` in C2 while compiling the existing `PolicyManager.getMaxPriority`. The retry ran the same 64-game protocol with that method excluded from JIT compilation on both sides. The interrupted sample was retained separately.

The clean retry completed 64 games in 11m 38.623s with 62 distinct map seeds and 64 unique game IDs. Strategy + City won 33, lost 31 and drew 0 (51.6%, 95% Wilson interval 39.6%–63.4%, two-sided exact p = 0.901). The net win-share delta was +3.1 pp (95% seed-clustered paired interval −21.2 to +27.3 pp). It won 18–14 when acting first and 15–17 when acting second. It won 25 Domination games (mean 176.0 turns, median 186) and eight Scientific games (mean 231.5 turns, median 228.5). M1 won 26 Domination games (mean 178.7 turns, median 181.5) and five Scientific games (mean 227.8 turns, median 225).

At turn 100 (63 pairs), the production delta was −0.73 [−13.21, +11.56], science per turn +3.39 [−13.42, +20.69], completed technologies +0.11 [−0.41, +0.63], population −0.63 [−4.55, +3.33], cities +0.14 [−0.27, +0.54], and average city population −0.45 [−1.15, +0.27]. The retry processed 12,038 game turns at 17.23 turns/s. The large observed production and win deficits of full M2 did not recur in the city-only screen. The smaller sample and separate maps limit attribution to the technology planner or its interaction with construction. The screen did not establish a measurable development gain from Strategy + City by itself.

**M2 STATUS: INCOMPLETE.** The candidate missed the economic-development exit condition and reduced strength. Commit `fe0a89d7b63ed7836821cc04b84dde901afcfe33` rolled back all M2 decision code. The branch keeps the enhanced benchmark reporting, and commit `4589a6738697bc5018efbc2a5d533b76ff059396` preserves the complete rejected candidate and its focused tests for review.
